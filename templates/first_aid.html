Emergency Knowledge Base

Step-by-Step Animal First-Aid Guide

Life-saving immediate measures you can take safely before the veterinary rescue team arrives.

Severe Bleeding

Apply direct pressure with sterile gauze. Do not remove saturated pads—add more on top. Keep the dog warm and avoid tight tourniquets.

Broken Limbs / Fractures

Do not try to set or straighten bones. Move the dog onto a blanket or rigid board. Keep head elevated and transport gently.

Heatstroke

Move to shade immediately. Pour cool (not freezing) water over paws, neck, and belly. Offer small sips of water.

Gentle Restraint (Towel Wrap)

Injured cats panic and scratch. Wrap the cat gently like a burrito in a thick towel, exposing only the injured area for emergency inspection.

Choking / Airway Obstruction

Check mouth carefully without getting bitten. If an object is visible, use tweezers to dislodge it. Do not push foreign objects deeper.

Roadside Accident & Trauma

Cordon off oncoming traffic. Provide shade and clean water. Do not force a down cow to stand up. Alert large-animal veterinary rescue immediately.

Maggot Wounds / Lesions

Clean surrounding dirt with saline solution. Apply eucalyptus or neem oil spray to keep flies away until veterinary wound debridement.

Wing Injury & Manja Cuts

Place the bird in a cardboard box with air holes and soft cloth at the bottom. Keep in a dark, warm, silent room. Do not force water down its throat.
