Engineering ResQPaws AI

Transforming animal emergency rescue through automated computer vision, geospatial routing algorithms, and multi-tier medical dispatch.

The Problem: Lost Golden Hours in Animal Rescue

When pedestrians encounter injured stray animals on roads, they face major hurdles:

  • Unclear Location: Callers struggle to convey precise GPS pins to rescue hotlines.
  • Unknown Severity: Hospitals cannot assess whether a case requires an ICU trauma ambulance or simple outpatient wound dressing.
  • Bystander Helplessness: Citizens do not know proper first-aid and risk causing further harm or suffering animal bites.

ResQPaws AI solves all three instantly: accurate image triage, automatic GPS dispatch, and tailored first-aid instructions.

Technical Specifications
Computer Vision: YOLOv8 & OpenCV contour analysis
Backend: Django 4.2+, Python 3.10+, REST Architecture
Geo-Spatial Engine: Haversine Great-Circle Geocoding & OpenStreetMap
Frontend: HTML5, Modern CSS3, Bootstrap 5, AJAX
Triage Levels: Critical (Code Red), Severe (Amber), Moderate (Yellow), Minor (Green)
